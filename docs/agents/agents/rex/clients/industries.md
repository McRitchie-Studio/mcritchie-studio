## Confidentiality — read first

This client's deal, legal and financial material lives in the knowledge layer
behind auth, and **it does not come out**. Not into this file, not into an
artifact, not into any external service, not into a social post. Standing rule,

This dossier is therefore **marketing-only** and deliberately carries no
financials, no counterparties, no terms and no dates from the transaction. If

## The business

Commercial welding and fabrication in the Denver metro, under new ownership.

## Who buys

Other businesses: general contractors, fabricators, plant and facilities
managers, equipment owners with something broken or something to build. This is
trade B2B. The buyer has a job, a deadline and a tolerance for one phone call.

Three things follow, and they make this client different from the other two:

- **The buying window is narrow and reactive.** Nobody shops for a welder in
  advance. They need one the week something cracks, and they call whoever comes
  to mind or comes up first.
- **Trust is local and personal.** A referral from a foreman outperforms any ad.
- **Volume of customers is low; value per customer is high.** One recurring
  contractor relationship can be worth more than a hundred consumer signups.
  Consumer-style volume tactics do not transfer.

## The constraint, stated plainly

There is no marketing function at all. Historically this business ran on word of
mouth, which is a real channel but an uncontrollable one — it cannot be dialed up
on the week you need it to be.

Under the SOP's step-3 table this looks like row 1 (**measurement**) and row 3
(**volume**) at once, with an unusual property: for this client the cheapest
first win is almost certainly **capture, not creation**. A trade business with
existing word of mouth is usually already receiving demand it does not record.
Inbound calls that nobody logged, quotes that nobody followed up, a past-customer
list nobody has ever contacted twice.

Before making anything new, find out what is already arriving.

## What is NOT instrumented

Unknown, and that is the first job. Specifically unknown: whether inbound calls
are logged anywhere, whether past customers exist as a list, whether the business
appears in local search, and whether anyone has ever asked a satisfied customer
for a referral on purpose.

## Numbers Rex needs (currently unknown)

| # | Question | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inbound job enquiries per week, and how each one arrived | The whole diagnosis. Also the number most likely to be unrecorded |
| 2 | Quotes issued versus jobs won | Whether the constraint is demand or closing |
| 3 | Average job value, and whether customers return | Sets what a customer is worth and whether retention is the lever |
| 4 | Does a customer list exist, in any form, even paper? | The cheapest demand in the building |
| 5 | Does the business appear in local search for its trade and area? | Reactive buyers search; being absent is a silent leak |
| 6 | Who answers the phone, and what happens when nobody does? | In trade B2B this is a marketing question, not an ops one |

## The handoff constraint nobody should forget

This client's marketing capacity is not Alex's attention — it is the
shop's. A plan that requires the owner-operator to produce content weekly will
not survive contact with a busy fabrication floor. **Prescribe things that run
without the shop's daily involvement**, or prescribe nothing.
