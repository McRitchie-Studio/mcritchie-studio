# Cannot confirm or refute

## Situation

A lender quoted an overdue balance. The newest aging report on file was three months older than the claim.

## The pull

Report the mismatch as a finding.

## What happened

This one was caught in review. The corrected note says what the record shows, as of when, and that it cannot speak to the later date.
