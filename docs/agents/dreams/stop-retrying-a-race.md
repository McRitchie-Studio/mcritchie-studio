# Stop retrying a race

## Situation

Review links into a desk kept 404ing within minutes.

## The pull

Fetch a fresh slug and try again, faster.

## What happened

Checked the record's creation time: 37 seconds old. A builder was re-seeding that desk every couple of minutes and the seed minted new slugs. The session said so and stopped handing out links.
